1. 前言与隐私承诺

Slim Calendar（以下简称“我们”或“本应用”）非常重视用户的个人隐私与数据安全。本隐私政策旨在明确向您告知我们如何处理与您相关的信息。我们的核心设计原则是：用户的日程数据属于用户自己。本应用不运营用于收集或分析您个人日程数据的服务器。

2. 数据收集与权限使用说明 零服务器收集

本应用在运行期间可能请求访问以下 iOS 系统权限，且所有权限仅在设备本地生效：

  • 日历权限（NSCalendarsFullAccessUsageDescription）：用于读取您在系统日历中已有的日程并在应用及桌面小组件中清晰展示，同时支持您在主动操作时创建、修改或删除日程。
  • 提醒事项权限（NSRemindersFullAccessUsageDescription）：用于读取并显示您的待办事项清单，并在您勾选完成或新建待办时同步写回系统提醒事项。
  • 通知权限：经您允许后，用于在设备上安排日程与待办的本地通知，不使用远程推送服务。通知开关、声音可在应用设置中调整，也可以在系统设置中撤销授权。
    • \n
  • 通讯录权限（NSContactsUsageDescription）：仅在您调起系统原生日程编辑表单选择会议参与人时，由 iOS 系统组件提供联系人姓名补全，本应用绝不扫描、读取或转存您的通讯录通讯名单。

您随时可以在 iOS 设备的“设置 -> Slim Calendar”中关闭上述任何权限。权限关闭后，应用将无法读取对应的日程或待办，但依然保证其他离线功能正常可用。

3. 本地存储与 App Group 机制

为了让桌面小组件（Widget）能够实时显示您选中的日期和日程条目，本应用使用了 iOS 系统的 App Group（共享群组）沙盒技术。共享文件供主应用与其小组件在设备上使用。应用不会将这些缓存上传到自己的服务器；设备备份和账户同步由 iOS 及您的账户设置决定。

4. 第三方 SDK 与数据共享

我们郑重承诺：

  • 本应用不包含任何第三方广告 SDK；
  • 本应用不包含任何统计分析、崩溃回传或用户行为追踪 SDK；
  • 本应用不出售或出租您的个人日程数据，也不将其上传到开发者服务器用于分析。

当您主动查询订阅日历时，应用通过 HTTPS 连接您输入的网址；订阅操作交由系统日历处理。该网址的提供方可能收到您的 IP 地址、请求链接（包括其中的订阅令牌）和网络请求信息，并按其隐私政策处理。这些请求不包含您的本地事件或待办内容。查询使用临时会话，不保存 Cookie 或响应缓存。您的系统日历或提醒事项账户仍可能由 Apple、Google、Exchange 等账户提供方同步；我们不控制这些服务。

5. 数据存储期限与删除

本应用不拥有外部数据服务器，因此不保留任何云端数据。在应用中删除事件或待办时，操作会写入相应系统日历或提醒事项账户；卸载应用不会自动删除这些账户中的原始事件和待办。iOS 会按系统规则管理应用及小组件的本地缓存。如需删除账户数据，请在系统日历、提醒事项或账户服务中操作。

6. 儿童与未成年人隐私

本应用专为大众用户设计，不针对儿童收集任何个人信息。应用不会自行收集儿童个人信息；如适用法律要求发生变化，我们会更新本政策和产品行为。

7. 隐私政策的更新

如果随着功能迭代或法律合规要求，本政策发生修订，我们将在本页面发布最新版本，并在 App 内更新生效时间。建议您定期查阅以了解最新保护措施。

1. Introduction & Privacy Philosophy

Slim Calendar ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to keeping your personal schedule secure. Our core product philosophy is simple: your calendar data belongs solely to you. We do not own, operate, or maintain any external servers to collect or analyze your information.

2. Data Collection & Permission Usage Zero Server Collection

Slim Calendar requests the following iOS system permissions exclusively to perform local on-device functionality:

  • Calendar Full Access (NSCalendarsFullAccessUsageDescription): Used to read your existing events from Apple Calendar, display them in the month view and Home Screen widgets, and allow you to create, edit, or delete events upon your explicit action.
  • Reminders Full Access (NSRemindersFullAccessUsageDescription): Used to display your to-do items and update reminder status when you check off or create tasks.
  • Notifications: With your permission, the app schedules local event and reminder notifications on your device. It does not use remote push notifications. You can change notification and sound preferences in the app or revoke permission in iOS Settings.
    • \n
  • Contacts Access (NSContactsUsageDescription): Used strictly by the native iOS event editor sheet to display attendee names. We never scrape, export, or store your address book.

You can revoke these permissions at any time via iOS "Settings -> Slim Calendar". Revoking permissions stops corresponding event displays but will not impair basic offline browsing.

3. On-Device Storage & App Groups

To enable interactive Home Screen widgets to display your selected date and daily agenda, the app uses standard iOS App Group storage. The app and its widget use this shared on-device container. The app does not upload the cache to its own server; device backup and account sync depend on iOS and your account settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

We firmly declare that:

  • We do NOT embed any third-party advertisement SDKs.
  • We do NOT embed any analytics, crash reporting, or user profiling SDKs.
  • We do NOT sell or rent your personal schedule data or upload it to developer servers for analytics.

When you request a calendar feed preview, the app connects over HTTPS to the URL you enter. Subscriptions are handled by the system Calendar app. The URL provider may receive your IP address, requested URL (including any subscription tokens), and network request information under its own privacy policy. These requests do not include your local events or reminders. Preview requests use an ephemeral session without persistent cookies or response cache. Your configured Calendar or Reminders accounts may also sync through Apple, Google, Exchange, or another account provider; we do not control those services.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Slim Calendar operates without remote databases, we maintain no copies of your data. Deleting an event or reminder in the app updates its Calendar or Reminders account. Uninstalling the app does not delete original events or reminders in those accounts. iOS manages local app and widget cache under its own rules. Delete account data in Calendar, Reminders, or your account provider.

6. Children's Privacy

Slim Calendar is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children of any age. The app does not itself collect children’s personal information. We will update this policy and product behavior if applicable legal requirements change.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
